## DESIGN NOTES (For Developers)

- **Color Palette:** Warm, earthy tones (rust, cream, sage green) + accent colors (gold, warm orange)—feels premium but relatable, not corporate
- **Typography:** Sans-serif for headings (modern, clean), serif for body copy (readable, trustworthy)
- **CTA Buttons:** Warm orange/gold, large touch targets (mobile-first), rounded corners (friendly, not corporate)
- **Testimonials:** Include photos of real market women, authentic quotes, visible profit improvements
- **Social Proof:** Show actual numbers (2,000+ users, ₦150M tracked inventory, 98% would recommend)
- **Mobile-First:** Entire page optimized for phone (most users access via phone)
- **Loading:** Fast (images optimized, lazy-loaded)
- **Accessibility:** High contrast, alt text on all images, proper heading hierarchy

---

- ✅ Clear value proposition (first 5 seconds)
